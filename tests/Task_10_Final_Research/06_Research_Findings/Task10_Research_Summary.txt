TASK 10.4 - ADAPTIVE SENSOR WEIGHTING
Weights calculated from normalized quality scores.

Observed behavior:
- Camera weight decreased during Camera degradation.
- LiDAR weight decreased during LiDAR degradation.
- Radar weight reached zero during injected Radar failure.
- Remaining sensor weights were redistributed.
- MATLAB weight-normalization checks passed.
- No all-sensor-failure samples occurred.

RESEARCH CONCLUSION:
The prototype successfully responds to controlled
degradation of its own input quality measurements
and dynamically reallocates sensor weights.

LIMITATIONS:
1. Sensor faults were synthetically injected into metrics.
2. Weights have not been calibrated against detection errors.
3. No object-level sensor fusion has been implemented.
4. No improvement in object detection has yet been demonstrated.
5. Further evaluation requires independent Ground Truth
   and comparable scenarios.
