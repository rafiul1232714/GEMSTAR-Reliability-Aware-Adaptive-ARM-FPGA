2. FIRST FPGA KERNEL

3. NUMERICAL VALIDATION
Start with INT16 XYZ candidate precision.
Calibrate scales on separate frames/recordings.
Use independent frames for validation.
Validate plane coefficients and arithmetic precision.

4. HARDWARE INTEGRATION
Select actual ARM-FPGA board and toolchain.
Specify AXI stream/control and DMA requirements.
Confirm clock frequency, memory and resource limits.
Use identical input data for CPU and FPGA comparison.

5. MEASUREMENTS
Measure software, DMA, accelerator and total latency.
Measure real board power with appropriate instrumentation.
Energy per frame = measured energy over the frame interval.
Account for idle baseline and system-level power.
Report LUT, FF, BRAM and DSP utilization from implementation.

6. RESEARCH LIMITATIONS
