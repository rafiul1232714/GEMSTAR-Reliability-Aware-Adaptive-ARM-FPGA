RESEARCH FINDINGS AND LIMITATIONS
- YOLO11s is the highest-duration tested workload.
- Radar valid-return processing is lightweight.
- LiDAR filtering takes longer than clustering in this selected scenario.
- EKF is timed per 24-observation replay, not per sensor frame.
- Measurements have different timing units and cannot be summed into a pipeline latency.
- Some comparisons use different images and software environments.
- p99 estimates are exploratory; YOLO has only ten runs per image.
- No final ARM CPU performance was measured.
- No FPGA performance or board energy was measured.
- Perception quality must remain a hard constraint.
- Hardware allocation is provisional until ARM/FPGA measurements.
