Fog images showed higher brightness and lower contrast/edge strength
in the five sampled images. Scene matching was not exact.

Pre-fix: 794 frames, positive return rate 45.082%.
Post-fix: 705 frames, positive return rate 18.966%.

Pre-fix maximum positive LiDAR distance: 20.006 m.
Post-fix maximum positive LiDAR distance: 6.927 m.

Post-fix positive returns beyond 7 m: 0.
Post-fix positive returns beyond 10 m: 0.

3. Radar:
The inspected simple Radar implementation did not show
weather-dependent attenuation. Further validation is needed.

over the common compared interval.
