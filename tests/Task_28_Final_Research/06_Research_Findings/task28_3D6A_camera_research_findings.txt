RESEARCH INTERPRETATION
- ROI source area is 36 percent of full pixels.
- YOLO requested inference size remained 640.
- Reduced input area did not guarantee lower YOLO latency.
- FULL and ROI produce different detection sets.
- ROI matching is detector consistency, not accuracy.
- ROI preprocessing and YOLO inference are distinct costs.

LIMITATIONS
- Only 20 images, five per recording.
- Scenario images are not proven matched scenes.
- CPU benchmark is not KR260 hardware timing.
- No measured energy or independent object accuracy.
- RGB convention follows Task 24; source not verified.
- Repeat timing observations are not independent images.
- No power or energy saving has been established.
